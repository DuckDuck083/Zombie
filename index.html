Red Dot Outbreak

Bird's-eye zombie shooter

Move or point the mouse to aim. Your gun sprays in the direction you face, ammo boxes keep you firing, and coins buy weapons, upgrades, and mercenaries.

W A S D or arrows to move. Mouse turns. Right click uses your secondary. R reloads. Q uses coin upgrades. Press Space to start or restart.
